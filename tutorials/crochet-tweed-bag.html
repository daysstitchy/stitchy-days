How to Crochet a Tweed Bag

Looking for a chic and stylish crochet project? This crochet tweed bag is the ultimate cute accessory for parties, nights out, or carrying daily essentials like your phone and lip gloss. It uses two strands of contrasting yarn held together to create a realistic tweed pattern and features a DIY sequin thread for a touch of handmade sparkle!

About the Pattern

Project Type: Tweed-Look Crochet Party Bag with Sequins

Technique: Alternating 2-row repeat (Tweed Double Crochet row + Sequined Single Crochet row) while carrying yarns.

Yarn & Thread Setup:

Pink Tweed Sections: 1 strand of light pink yarn + 1 strand of dark pink yarn held together.

White/Sequin Sections: White yarn + thread pre-strung with loose sequins held together.

Video Tutorial

Watch the full step-by-step video tutorial before starting, especially if you are a beginner.

Materials

  • Weight 3 acrylic yarn
  • Loose sequins
  • Sewing thread for threading sequins
  • 4 mm crochet hook
  • Metal chain strap
  • Magnetic snap buttons

Pattern Details

  • Skill Level: Easy / Intermediate
  • Time Required: 2–3 hours
  • Pattern Type: Bags & Purses
  • Stitches Used: Chain (ch), single crochet (sc), double crochet (dc), slip stitch (sl st), back loop only (BLO)

Abbreviations

ch Chain
sl st Slip stitch
sc Single crochet
dc Double crochet
BLO Back loop only

Exact Written Pattern

Prep

Thread Sequins:

Using a sewing needle, string loose sequins onto a separate thread prior to starting.

Yarn Management:

Do not cut yarns when switching colors; carry unused yarns along inside the stitches.

1. Front Panel

Foundation:

Chain 33 using the two pink yarns held together (31 base chains + 2 turning chains).

R1 (Tweed Row):

Skip first 2 chains.

Make 3 DC in pink.

Switch to White + Sequin thread on the last loop of the 3rd stitch, then make 1 DC in white.

Repeat [3 Pink DC, 1 White DC] across the entire row.

R2 (White/Sequin Row):

Switch to White yarn + Sequin thread.

Chain 1, turn.

Work SC in each stitch across while carrying the pink yarns along the top of your work.

Slide up a sequin from the thread every few stitches as desired.

R3–R10:

Repeat R1 and R2 alternately until you complete 10 rows total (5 tweed blocks).

Fasten off.

2. Back Panel & Flap

R1–R10:

Work the exact same pattern as the Front Panel (10 rows total).

R11 (Flap Fold Line):

Repeat Tweed Row pattern, but work into Back Loops Only (BLO) to create the fold edge.

R12–R14:

Continue repeating R2 and R1 pattern through both loops as normal.

R15:

Work into Back Loops Only (BLO) again.

R16–R22:

Work through both loops as normal, ending on Row 22.

Fasten off.

3. Side & Base Connecting Strip

Foundation:

Chain 9 (7 base chains + 2 turning chains) using the two pink yarns held together.

Pattern:

Skip first 2 chains.

Work in the same tweed repeat: 3 Pink DC, 1 White DC, 3 Pink DC.

Continue alternating with White SC rows until the strip is long enough to fit around both sides and the bottom of the bag.

4. Assembly & Finishing

Joining:

Using white yarn only (without sequin thread), slip stitch the connecting strip to the front and back panels:

Along vertical side rows:

Work 2 slip stitches into each DC row and 1 slip stitch into each SC row.

Along bottom stitches:

Slip stitch stitch-for-stitch, skipping every 3rd DC on the strip to align evenly.

Edging:

Slip stitch around all open edges (flap and opening) with white yarn for a clean border.

Closure & Strap:

Sew magnetic snap buttons onto the inside front panel and onto two small crocheted magic rings (12 SC each) on the flap.

Attach a metal purse chain strap to the top side corners.
