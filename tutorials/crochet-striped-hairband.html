How to Crochet an Easy Zigzag Striped Hairband | Beginner-Friendly Tutorial + Free Pattern (Step-by-Step)

This easy zigzag striped hairband is a beginner-friendly project that creates a beautiful, cozy texture while adding a bit of soft inspiration to your everyday style. The color changes make the zigzag stripes stand out beautifully, and the pattern is simpler than it looks.

About the Pattern

This zigzag striped hairband is one of my favorite dreamy crochet projects. It is very beginner-friendly and creates a cozy textured look that works beautifully with contrasting colors.

Please watch the step-by-step video tutorial alongside the written pattern for visual demonstrations of the zigzag texture and color switches.

Written Pattern

How to Crochet the Zigzag Hairband

1. The Foundation

Start by making a slip knot and creating a foundation chain that fits comfortably around your head.

I used 75 chains.

Ensure the chain count is compatible with the zigzag striped pattern repeat. Refer to 0:36 of the video for the exact multiple.

2. The Zigzag Body

Round 1: Ch 1 to start the height of your row, then begin the zigzag sequence by alternating increases and decreases after every 6 sc to create the peaks and valleys.

Follow this pattern for all the rows:

ch 1, (6 sc, inc, 6 sc, dec) × 5

To get the best striped effect, switch your yarn color at the end of the round or every few rounds.

Continue repeating the zigzag rows until the hairband reaches your desired width.

3. Finishing Touches

Once you are happy with the width, finish the final round with ch 1.

Fasten off your yarn and leave a small tail.

Use a yarn needle to weave in all your loose ends from the color changes to keep the headband looking clean and neat.

Style Tip

These look amazing in pastel “dreamy” colors or bold contrasting stripes!
